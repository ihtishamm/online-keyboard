Ko Ngā Motu Māhara  ( ) he whenua motu ki Ao-o-Kiwa.
Ko Terapa-Urika-Tihārata te tāone matua.
Ko te reo Māhara te reo rawa nui o te whenua.
Ko Hūmārie   (reo Homari: Jamhuuriyadda Federaalka Soomaaliya; reo Ārapi: جمهورية الصومال الفيدرالية), he whenua i Āwherika.
Ko Mogadishu te tāone matua me te tāone nui.
Ko Te Tai Rei   (Côte d'Ivoire) he whenua i Āwherika.
I tapā te ingoa nō te mea i ngā wā o mua ka hokohokona i reira he rei arewhana (ivory).
Waihoki, i korā ngā whenua ‘Te Tai Kōura’ me ‘Te Tai Taurekareka’.
Ko Nabopolassar i te kaitīmata me te tahi kīngi o te Emepaea Papurona hou.
E whakahaere tikanga ana nō 626 BC āpānoa 605 BC ua i mate ia.
Ko Paprotno ([paˈprɔtnɔ]; reo Tiamana: Parpart) tētahi tāone i te taiwhenua whakahaere o Gmina Karnice, i te Gryfice Kaute, Pomeranian-ki-te-uru Voivodeship, i Pōrana-ki-te-raki-mā-uru.
Ko te tāone 5 kiromita (3 mi) ki te tonga o Karnice, 13 km (8 mi) ki te raki-mā-uru o Gryfice, me 72 km (45 mi) ki te raki-mā-rawhiti o te tāone matua o te rohe; Szczecin.
Ko 260 te taupori o te tāone.
Ko Tinana te waka tupuna o Ngāpuhi / Ngāti Kahu ki Whaingaroa, o Ngāpuhi and Ngāti Kahu.
Ko Ngāti Porou ki Hauraki / Ngati Porou ki Harataunga ki Mataora he iwi nui, nō Hauraki, Te Ika-a-Māui, Aotearoa.
Ko te heamana ko o Ngāti Porou ki Hauraki ko John Tamihere, ko tana rongonui ko tana whawhai mō tā rātau tika me kereima ki kia rua ngā wāhi kai Hauraki.
E rua ngā peka o te iwi, ko Ngāti Porou ki Harataunga, kātahi ko Ngāti Porou ki Mataora.
Ko te whakapapa o tēnei iwi e hono ana rātau ki ngā tātai o Paikea-moana-ariki rātau ko Māui ko Toi.
Ka hoki ngā mahara ki te taenga mai o Paikea ki Ahuahu me tana marenatanga ki a Te Āhurumōwairaka, ko Ngāti Porou ki Hauraki, ahakoa ko te iwi pōtiki kai Hauraki e noho ana, e pupuritia ana rātau ki wētahi hononga ki a Hauraki nō tuawhakarere, nō mua i ngā waka hekenga.
Ko te niho he mea koi me maro kei runga te waha hei ngangau kai kia ngāwari ki te horo.
Ka mahia hoki te kupu "niho" ki mata koi i tētahi māripi.
Ko Poronga (reo Itāria: [boˈloɲɲa]  ⓘ ; reo Emilian: Bulåggna [buˈlʌɲɲɐ]; reo Rātini: Bononia) he tāone nui ki Itāria.
Ka tāwhia te roto mā te wai mai ngā awa, o mai te rewa o ngā kōpaka.
Ko kia kaha he kīanga Māori e whakamahia whānuitia ana e te iwi o Aotearoa hei kupu whakakaha, arā, kia ū, kia manawanui.
He tino tikanga tō tēnei kīanga ki te Māori: i rongonui ai nā te whakamahinga a te 28th Māori Battalion i te Pakanga Tuarua o te Ao, ā, kei roto hoki i ngā taitara pukapuka, waiata, me ngā pepeha.
I te taha reo, e rua ngā wāhanga o kia kaha: ko kia, he kupu ā-wairua e tohu ana i te hiahia kia tutuki tētahi āhuatanga; ā, ko kaha, arā, te pakari, te kaha rānei.
Ko kaha nō te kupu Proto-Polynesian kafa, e tohu ana i te “pakari” me te “nui”; ko kafa anō hoki te kupu o taua wā mō te taura kaha i whatua mai i te muka kokonati (hei tauira, i te reo Hawaiʻi: ʻaha), he taura i whakamahia hei here waka, hei here rākau, hei here whare hoki.
